# Direction A | Tournament poster

A screen print that moves: flat inks, a banded dusk sky, a sun disc, thick
rules, light grain. Text sits on solid blocks, so the block is the scrim.

## Type (both OFL)
- **Big Shoulders Display 900:** heavy condensed signage caps for names,
  codes, scores, calls and buttons.
- **Barlow 500, 600, 700:** a road-sign grotesque with soft terminals that
  keeps the body warm. Tabular numerals.

## Colour per role
Ink `#1B1B1E`, Cream `#F3E9D2`, Clay `#D9622B` (primary action, or the server
mark in play), Teal `#0F4C5C` (court, phone ground), Ball `#D8EF3A` (ball,
timing marker, ripple). Secondary text: `#BDB5A3` on ink, `#5A5344` on cream,
`#B4CDCB` on teal. Scene only: sky `#3A2147`, `#6A2F5E`, `#B94A68`, `#E77C59`,
`#F6B56C`; sun `#FCE0A2`; ground `#9A4527`; stand `#2B1B3A`. Racket colours:
apricot `#F2A65A`, rose `#E2788A`, mint `#7CC6B8`, plum `#9A6BB0`. Sides also
differ by shirt: plain near, hooped far.

## Measured contrast
Cream on ink 14.24 (13.11 rendered with grain). Cream on teal 7.88.
Secondary on ink 8.43, on cream 6.31, on teal 5.68. Ink on clay 4.69 (4.51
rendered). Ink title on sky, rendered: rose 3.28, coral 5.67, peach
8.90 (large text). Ball on teal 7.39. Figures on court: 3.30 at worst.
Court against ground is only 1.47; the cream lines carry that edge.

## Type scale
TV at 1920 x 1080: body and labels 28 px, names 58 px, score digits 78 px,
room code 186 px, call 264 px, title 266 px. Phone: body 17 px, labels 16 px,
headings 54 px, buttons 34 to 40 px, controls at least 52 px tall.

## Art direction for the 3D scene
Unlit or two-step toon shading, no gradients, no bloom. Five hard sky bands,
one flat sun behind the stand, long flat shadows toward the camera. Crowd as
rows of coloured dots, never faces. Figures are pills with a cream headband
and an oversized racket. Floodlight grids light cell by cell as the rally
grows. The second split view faces away from the sun: plum sky.

## Next

## Weakest point
The lobby carries a lot of 28 px text and reads closer to a schedule than a
poster. The flat court also sets a bar the 3D render must match, or HUD and
scene will feel like two styles.
